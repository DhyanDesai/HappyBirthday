### Memory 1 — The birthday wish on Telegram

- **When:** Around Dhyan's birthday; exact date and year not yet provided.
- **Where it began:** Telegram. This was Dhyan and Gunjan's first real interaction: Gunjan wished him a happy birthday.
- **What happened next:** They gradually moved their conversations to WhatsApp.
- **Dhyan's first impression:** Gunjan seemed strict and very serious about her studies. He worried that if he disturbed her while she was studying, she might stop talking to him.
- **Dhyan's own words to preserve:** “Me aa chhokri ne study ma disturb kari to aa mari sathe vaaat nai kare...”
- **Why it matters:** Dhyan describes this as their first and most important memory, the beginning of their friendship. At the time, he had rigid ideas about what friendship had to look like. Gunjan showed him that a friendship can be beautiful without those restrictions.
- **Dhyan's reflection, in his words:** “I used to believe that friendship means this... aavu to hovu j joiye and aavu nai thay to nai chale... but you were the one who taught me that without any restrictions also friendships can be so so so beautiful.”
